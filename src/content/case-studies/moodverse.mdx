<Section n="01" k="Context" title="The problem">

People often know exactly how they feel but not which words to search for. A general-purpose LLM can meet them in natural language, but on its own it can also produce text that sounds like scripture and isn’t. In a spiritual product, an invented verse isn’t a minor bug. It breaks trust completely.

</Section>

<Section n="02" k="Design" title="The architecture and decisions">

I split the problem in two. Interpretation belongs to the model: Gemini reads the reflection and returns structured emotional themes. The text belongs to retrieval: semantic search with pgvector over a curated, verified corpus. Anything shown as scripture comes from a stored passage, never from generated output.

- FastAPI with Pydantic v2 for typed request and response contracts
- Supabase PostgreSQL with SQLAlchemy 2.x and Alembic migrations
- pgvector keeps embeddings next to the source passages, with no separate vector store to sync
- React Native client for a mobile-first reflection flow

</Section>
